# Marketing reference adaptation

The ScholarMatch homepage was compared with the public Wispr Flow homepage as it appeared on 28 July 2026. The implementation borrows a visual rhythm, not brand content or assets.

## Shared visual language

- Warm ivory page canvas with a compact inset navigation bar.
- Oversized serif display type paired with compact sans-serif utility copy.
- Lavender, ink-bordered calls to action.
- Long editorial spacing followed by large rounded dark product demonstrations.
- Alternating light and dark story sections, horizontal proof movement, pill-shaped tabs, and a large closing call to action.
- Restrained motion that supports hierarchy and becomes static under reduced-motion preferences.

## Deliberate ScholarMatch differences

- The hero animation is an original HTML/CSS matching pipeline rather than a voice or waveform motif.
- The dark product demonstration shows profile facts, verified eligibility checks, and ranked match explanations; it contains no Wispr interface, copy, or media.
- The proof band uses non-claim scholarship categories instead of company or partner logos.
- The outcome section compares search-first and match-first discovery without publishing numerical performance claims.
- Use-case tabs are organized around student study paths rather than professions or work roles.
- Feature stories focus on eligibility, explainability, deadlines, and document readiness.
- Testimonials are not populated until approved, attributable ScholarMatch stories exist. Production displays an explicit empty state.
- The page does not reproduce the reference mascot, illustrations, app icons, screenshots, testimonials, statistics, downloadable assets, or source code.
